Our Story

Welcome to Shawarma Wrap, where the rich culinary traditions of Syria come alive in every bite. Our journey began with a passion for sharing the authentic flavors of Damascus – from perfectly spiced shawarma wraps to our signature traditional dishes.


We use only the finest ingredients and time-honored recipes passed down through generations. Each dish is crafted with love and respect for Syrian heritage, bringing you an authentic taste of the Middle East right here in your neighborhood.


From our family to yours, we invite you to experience the warmth, hospitality, and incredible flavors that make Syrian cuisine truly special.
